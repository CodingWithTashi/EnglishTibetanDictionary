Losar greetings across the plateau
Tibetan Times · 3 min read
On the first morning of the new year, families rise before dawn to exchange the greeting བཀྲ་ཤིས་བདེ་ལེགས། and offer the first bowl of chang to the household shrine.
Khapse pastries are stacked in tall towers, and the དེར་ཁ། box of roasted barley and wheat is passed to each guest.

Identity evolves. The old brown/gold becomes one maroon accent on a warm off-white, with saffron-tinted tonal surfaces instead of shadows. Dark mode is warm charcoal.

Bilingual UI. Labels show English with a small Tibetan gloss; the app-language setting flips which is primary.
