Privacy Policy

Tibetan Dictionary for Android · Effective [Month DD, 2026]

  • The dictionary works fully offline. You can use every feature without an account.
  • Searches, saved words and history stay on your device unless you choose to sign in.
  • If you sign in, we store only what is needed to sync your saved words, history and settings.
  • We do not sell your data or show personalised ads.

1. Who we are

Tibetan Dictionary ("the app") is published by [Developer / Company Name] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how. Questions can be sent to [privacy@example.com].

2. Using the app without an account

All 180,000+ dictionary entries are bundled with the app and searched on your device. When you are not signed in:

  • Your search terms, search history, saved words, learning progress and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • We do not receive this information.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it permanently.

3. Using the app with an account (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account to keep your words on more than one device. When you do, we receive and store:

  • Account details: your email address, name and profile picture as provided by Google Sign-In, and a unique account ID.
  • Sync data: your saved words and lists, search history, learning progress and app settings.
  • Sync metadata: the time of your last sync and the device it came from.

This data is used only to sync your content between your devices. We do not read your Google contacts, files or any other Google account data. Sync data is stored with [Google Firebase / hosting provider], which processes it on our behalf.

4. Notifications

If you turn on the Word of the Day notification, the app schedules it locally on your device at the time you choose. No personal information is sent to schedule or deliver it. You can turn it off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

5. Diagnostics

[If used:] To find and fix bugs, the app may send anonymous crash reports and basic technical information (app version, Android version, device model) through [Firebase Crashlytics / provider]. These reports do not contain your searches, saved words or account details. [If not used, delete this section.]

6. Permissions

  • Internet: used only for sign-in, sync and downloading dictionary updates. The dictionary itself works without it.
  • Notifications: used only for the optional Word of the Day.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with the service providers named above, who process it on our behalf, or when required by law. When you use "Share" on an entry or the app, the content goes to the app you pick, under that app's own privacy policy.

8. Advertising

The app does not show ads and does not use advertising identifiers.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data stays on your device until you clear it, delete it in the app, or uninstall.
  • Synced data is kept while your account is active.
  • You can delete your account and all synced data from Profile → Account → Delete account, or by emailing [privacy@example.com]. We complete deletion within 30 days.
  • Signing out stops sync but keeps a copy of your data on that device.

10. Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Synced data is protected so that only your signed-in account can access it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To make a request, contact [privacy@example.com]. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12. Children

The app is suitable for learners of all ages and can be used without an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
